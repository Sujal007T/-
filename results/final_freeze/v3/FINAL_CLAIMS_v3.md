# FINAL CLAIMS (Freeze v3)

This document establishes the verified scientific claims for the DisaSense project. Every statement is derived strictly from empirical frozen numbers and cites its exact source file. In accordance with rigorous auditing constraints, all language avoids unverified assertions of causality or consistency, confines report-level lexical evaluation to unified unstemmed metrics, reports cluster bootstrap intervals, and explicitly details negative controls and failure modes.

---

## 1. Supported Claims

### Track A: Building Damage Assessment (xBD)

1. On the clean UID test intersection ($N = 3,982$), Baseline A (ResNet50) achieves an accuracy of 71.75% and a macro-F1 of 0.6764, while Baseline B (ChangeDetection) achieves 71.30% accuracy and a macro-F1 of 0.6597. *(Source: `results/final_freeze/v3/v3_analysis_summary.json`)*
2. On the same clean UID test set ($N = 3,982$), the validation-tuned $\{A, B\}$ ensemble (weights 0.75 / 0.25) yields an accuracy of 72.25% and a macro-F1 of 0.6810. *(Source: `results/final_freeze/v3/v3_analysis_summary.json`)*
3. In paired cluster bootstrap resampling across the 184 test image pairs containing clean buildings (1,000 resamples, seed 42), the $\{A, B\}$ ensemble achieves a mean macro-F1 difference of +0.0045 with a 95% bootstrap confidence interval of $[-0.0026, +0.0118]$ and an accuracy difference of +0.51% with a 95% confidence interval of $[-0.19\%, +1.16\%]$ against Baseline A; because both cluster confidence intervals span zero, the ensemble gain is within the noise of Baseline A at the image-pair cluster level. *(Source: `results/final_freeze/v3/v3_analysis_summary.json`)*
4. Zero-shot BLIP-2 (Baseline C) achieves a test accuracy of 43.22% and a macro-F1 of 0.2110, which performs only slightly above the trivial "always no-damage" floor baseline (macro-F1 of 0.2014) and below a random class-prior sampler drawn from training frequencies (mean macro-F1 of 0.2475). *(Source: `results/final_freeze/v3/v3_analysis_summary.json`)*
5. The 163 boundary-clipped test patches (3.93% of the initial 4,145 test buildings) exhibit a class distribution (65.03% no-damage, 12.88% minor-damage, 15.34% major-damage, 6.75% destroyed) that closely matches the 3,982 clean patches (67.45% no-damage, 14.29% minor-damage, 11.53% major-damage, 6.73% destroyed). *(Source: `results/final_freeze/v3/v3_analysis_summary.json`)*
6. In a CPU re-inference spot check on 200 seeded clean test UIDs, both Baseline A and Baseline B checkpoints achieve 100.0% argmax agreement with saved test probabilities, exhibiting maximum absolute differences of $3.37 \times 10^{-6}$ and $7.57 \times 10^{-6}$ respectively. *(Source: `results/final_freeze/v3/v3_analysis_summary.json`)*
7. The saved predictions and probabilities in `baseline_c_predictions.csv` originate entirely from the current heuristic parser in `src/models/baseline_c_vlm.py`, which achieves 100.0% label agreement (4,145 / 4,145) and zero probability discrepancy against the saved file by handling prompt echo. *(Source: `results/final_freeze/v3/v3_analysis_summary.json`)*

### Track B: Disaster Type Recognition (DTR) & Report Captioning (DisasterM3)

8. Fine-tuned Condition D achieves 60.0% accuracy (120/200, 95% exact binomial CI: [52.9%, 66.8%]) on benchmark Disaster Type Recognition, outperforming zero-shot Condition C (43.5%, exact McNemar $p = 2.72 \times 10^{-5}$) and zero-shot Condition B (48.5%, exact McNemar $p = 0.0067$). *(Source: `results/final_freeze/v2/v2_analysis_summary.json`)*
9. Across the 30 benchmark DTR events, an event-level bootstrap test (1,000 resamples, seed 42) indicates Condition D exceeds Condition C by a mean accuracy difference of +16.86% [95% CI: +4.16%, +32.34%], achieving higher accuracy in 99.6% of event resamples. *(Source: `results/final_freeze/v2/v2_analysis_summary.json`)*
10. Across the 8 disaster classes, step-down Holm-Bonferroni correction ($m=8$) confirms statistically significant gains for Condition D over Condition C on `hurricane` ($+69.0\%$, adjusted $p = 1.53 \times 10^{-5}$), `earthquake` ($+36.7\%$, adjusted $p = 0.0239$), and `fire` ($+37.5\%$, adjusted $p = 0.0469$). *(Source: `results/final_freeze/v2/v2_analysis_summary.json`)*
11. Under a standardized unstemmed `rouge-score` metric (mean per-sample F-measure), report-level ROUGE-L on 200 benchmark caption samples is 0.3429 [95% CI: 0.3343, 0.3522] for Condition D, compared to 0.3181 [95% CI: 0.3096, 0.3269] for Condition D_blank, 0.3067 [95% CI: 0.3014, 0.3123] for Condition K, 0.3042 [95% CI: 0.2947, 0.3137] for Condition D_shuffled, and 0.2345 [95% CI: 0.2291, 0.2392] for Condition C. *(Source: `results/final_freeze/v3/v3_analysis_summary.json`)*
12. Under standardized SacreBLEU, report-level BLEU-4 on the 200 benchmark caption samples is 0.1821 [95% CI: 0.1737, 0.1899] for Condition D, compared to 0.1703 [95% CI: 0.1618, 0.1795] for Condition D_blank, 0.1601 [95% CI: 0.1547, 0.1656] for Condition K, 0.1511 [95% CI: 0.1435, 0.1590] for Condition D_shuffled, and 0.0512 [95% CI: 0.0488, 0.0538] for Condition C. *(Source: `results/final_freeze/v3/v3_analysis_summary.json`)*
13. Schema compliance for damage report generation is 100.0% (200/200) for Condition B, Condition D, Condition K, and Condition D_blank, while Condition C exhibits 98.5% compliance (197/200, with 3 samples truncated by repetitive token loops) and Condition D_shuffled exhibits 98.5% compliance (197/200, with 3 samples suffering empty exclamation repetition). *(Source: `results/final_freeze/v3/v3_analysis_summary.json`)*

## 2. Not Supported / Refuted Claims

1. **NOT SUPPORTED: Out-of-Event Generalization on xBD**: In the xBD split, 100.0% of test image pairs (281/281) and 100.0% of validation image pairs (279/279) originate from disaster events present in the training set; the split represents a spatial tile interpolation, not an evaluation on unseen disaster events. *(Source: `results/final_freeze/v2/v2_analysis_summary.json`)*
2. **NOT SUPPORTED: Spatial Delta Change-Detection in Condition D**: When evaluated on same-disaster shuffled image pairs (where pre- and post-images originate from different geographical coordinates within the same event), Condition D achieves 55.0% DTR accuracy, which is not significantly lower than genuine paired imagery (60.0%, exact McNemar $p = 0.237$), indicating that performance cannot be separated from event and scene familiarity. *(Source: `results/track_b_v2/d_matched_controls/matched_controls_report.md`)*
3. **NOT SUPPORTED: Robust Recognition of Rare Tail Disasters**: Under the 250-step training budget (batch size 8, representing ~0.27 effective epochs over DTR samples), Condition D fails on rare tail classes, scoring 0.0% accuracy on volcano events (0/13) and 14.3% on explosion events (3/21). *(Source: `results/track_b_v2/final/main_table.md`)*
4. **NOT SUPPORTED: Validated Automated Section-Level Damage Classification**: All entries in the human verification columns of `results/track_b/labeler_validation.csv` are null (0 human annotations), and automated rule labelers for road and vegetation collapsed entirely to the 'intact' class; consequently, no section-level accuracy claims are supported. *(Source: `results/final_audit/part_09.md`)*
5. **NOT SUPPORTED: Independent Model Diversity in Track A Baselines**: Baseline B was initialized directly by warm-starting from `baseline_a_best.pt` rather than trained independently from ImageNet weights, reducing the theoretical parameter independence assumed in ensembling. *(Source: `results/final_audit/part_01.md`)*
